My research examines how trade and industrial policies influence natural resources and the environment, and vice versa.

I am currently doing my PhD at the [Center of Environmental Economics - Montpellier](https://www.cee-m.fr/), [University of Montpellier](https://economie.edu.umontpellier.fr/) & the [French National Institute for Industrial Environment and Risks](https://www.ineris.fr/fr), studying the externalities from primary and secondary production of critical raw materials.
